# Step 3 (optional): Google Chat reminders

Reviewers get a private Google Chat message **from you**. They install nothing.

**You need:** a Google Workspace account (work or school). Plain @gmail.com does **not** work.

Google requires a one-time, free "OAuth client" (about 5 minutes). Click **Sign in to Google Chat** and the extension walks you through it and opens the right Google pages. The full picture-by-step list is in the extension's README.

Then add each reviewer's work email: open the dashboard, find **Reviewer mapping**, and type `their-github-name` → `their-email@company.com`.

Finish with **PR Reminder: Test Google Chat Connection** to send yourself a test.
